Built for Paperwork Reality

Everything a local document vault should be

No proprietary database traps, no cloud OCR queues, and no per-page subscriptions. Just fast Apple Silicon AI working directly with your files.

On-Device MLX Vision AI

Choose from Gemma 4 models (E2B to 26B-A4B MoE) tuned to your Mac's RAM. Download in one click, and let idle TTL unloading free unified memory when you're done.

Disk-First Vault, Zero Lock-In

Your Finder folders are the single source of truth—non-year folders act as labels and 4-digit folders power Year filters. Stop using the app anytime and keep a spotless archive.

Drag-Drop & Folder Watcher

Drop PDFs, PNGs, or JPEGs anywhere on the window, or point the background Watcher at your scanner's Incoming folder so new pages file themselves automatically.

Auto-Rotate & Review Queue

Sideways scans are straightened automatically, and low-confidence or duplicate files pause in your Review Queue so you always have the final say before moving files.

Custom Masks & Multi-Vault

Customize folder tree masks, filename templates, date formats, and title language—and switch effortlessly between personal, family, or business vaults.

Instant Search & Previews

Backed by a fast local SQLite index and cached page thumbnails, Document Vault lets you filter, preview, rotate, rename, or reclassify thousands of documents in milliseconds.

Workflow

From messy inbox to calm archive in seconds

Lives quietly in your macOS menu bar and opens cleanly in your browser whenever you need it.

Drop or Watch

Drop PDFs or scanned photos into the browser, or let your flatbed/mobile scanner save into a watched Incoming folder.

2

Local Vision Reads & Names

The MLX Vision model reads page 1 locally, infers the issue date, matches your categories, and generates a clean human-readable title.

3

Filed on Disk & Searchable

High-confidence documents file straight into your Vault folder hierarchy; uncertain ones wait in Review. Every file stays readable in Finder forever.
