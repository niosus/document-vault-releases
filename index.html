Capabilities

Fast local AI. Plain Finder folders.

No proprietary database traps, no cloud OCR queues, and no per-page subscriptions.

On-Device MLX Vision

Run Gemma 4 models (E2B to 26B-A4B) tuned to your Mac's memory, with automatic idle unloading when you're done.

Disk-First Finder Archive

Your Finder folders are the single source of truth — non-year folders act as labels and 4-digit folders power Year filters.

Drop & Folder Watcher

Drop PDFs or photos onto the window, or watch your scanner's Incoming folder with auto-rotation and a Review Queue.

Instant Search & Custom Masks

Filter and preview thousands of pages in milliseconds via local SQLite, with custom path templates and multi-vault support.

✓ 0 bytes leave your Mac · ✓ Works 100% offline · ✓ Standard Finder folders & sidecars
